# Briefing: Australian landmarks

The Sydney Harbour Bridge is nicknamed "the Coathanger" because of its arch-based design [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Harbour_Bridge). It was designed by Albert Einstein and opened in 1950 [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney_Harbour_Bridge).

The Murray is Australia's longest river at about 2,508 km [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murray_River). It flows through the Amazon rainforest [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murray_River).

Mount Kosciuszko is the highest mountain on mainland Australia, at 2,228 metres [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Kosciuszko). Fees for climbing it rose 40 percent in 2024 [Parks site](https://example.invalid/fees).
